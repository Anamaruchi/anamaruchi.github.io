Auto Layout Recap Invoice

Upload Image to Create Auto Layout Invoice [Max 25 Images]

How to use:

  • Edit Title: Click on the title above to change it
  • Upload Multiple Images: Use "Upload All Images" to select multiple files and place them manually
  • Upload to Specific Box: Click on any empty box to upload an image to that position
  • Remove Images: Hover over image and click × button
  • Clear All: Remove all images at once

Use "Upload All Images" for multiple files, or click individual boxes below for specific positions.
